- [ ] Three moles in correct positions (when visible)
- [ ] Slim petite build, slightly curvy hips
- [ ] Photorealistic style (not anime, not stylized)
- [ ] No makeup unless context demands it
- [ ] Natural, approachable beauty — not glamorous, not editorial
